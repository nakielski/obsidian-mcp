# Qdrant vs Pinecone for RAG

For the [[Second Brain Agent]] I needed to choose a vector database.

## Qdrant (local, Docker)
- 100% GDPR-compliant
- €0 cost

## Pinecone (Managed Cloud)
- US-cloud, Cloud Act risk
- ~$70/month

## Decision
Qdrant. For sensitive vault data, sovereignty is non-negotiable.
